Section 1:Milky Way银河

Summer Milky Way: the Summer Triangle 夏季银河：大三角

Cherry Springs State Park, PA, USA 美国宾夕法尼亚州樱桃泉州立公园 · August 2026 2026年八月

iPhone · 24mm (equiv.) · f/1.78 · 10s · ISO 10000 iPhone · 24mm（等效）· f/1.78 · 10s · ISO 10000

Lying on the ground with friends at Cherry Springs State Park, asking ChatGPT as we went, we found the striking Summer Triangle of the summer Milky Way, formed by Vega, Altair and Deneb in the Great Rift, along with all sorts of other constellations. For the first time I made out the Andromeda Galaxy, so faint and hazy that you could take it for cloud or mist if you didn't look closely, and watched the Milky Way rise like a wisp of steam from the teapot of Sagittarius. Sadly the photo is too noisy to show much of the Milky Way's detail. Next time I hope to take a Milky Way photo that is closer to what the naked eye can see. 在樱桃泉州立公园和朋友们躺在地上，一边问ChatGPT一边找到了在大裂缝中的织女星，牛郎星，天津四构成的醒目的夏季银河大三角，以及其他各种各样的星座。第一次观察到了模模糊糊的，不仔细看会以为是云雾的仙女座星系，看到了银河像一道蒸汽一样从茶壶一样的人马座蒸腾起来。可惜照片里面噪点太多了，很难看清楚银河的细节。希望下次能拍到更像肉眼能观察到的银河照片。

Milky Way time-lapse 银河延时

Cherry Springs State Park, PA, USA 美国宾夕法尼亚州樱桃泉州立公园 · August 2026 2026年八月

DJI 大疆

A Milky Way time-lapse a friend shot on a DJI, from about 1:00 to 2:30 a.m. We watched the Milky Way rise from the horizon and slowly become an arch across the sky, covering everyone's heads. Looking toward the galactic center and imagining the Earth slowly making its way along a distant spiral arm was awe-inspiring. 朋友用大疆拍到的银河延时摄影，拍摄时间大约是凌晨一点到两点半。看到银河从地平线升起，到慢慢在天上成为一个拱形，罩在所有人头顶上。看着银河中心，想象着地球在遥远旋臂上慢慢地走着，非常震撼。

Section 2:Star trails星轨

Star trails from dew-soaked grass 沾满露水的草地上拍到的星轨

Cherry Springs State Park, PA, USA 美国宾夕法尼亚州樱桃泉州立公园 · August 2026 2026年八月

Ricoh GR II · 18.3mm · f/2.8 · 20s per frame · ISO 800 Ricoh GR II · 18.3mm · f/2.8 · 单张 20s · ISO 800

My first star-trail photo. I set up a tripod near where we were staying and let the camera shoot on its own for about four hours. The next morning I found the camera covered in dew — next time, for winter star trails, I need to remember to bring a rain cover. 第一次拍摄星轨照片，在住宿的地方附近架起了三脚架，让相机自动抓拍了大约四个小时。第二天起来发现相机沾满了露水，下次拍冬季星轨要记得带一个防水罩。

Section 3:Meteors流星

Perseid meteor (probably) 英仙座流星（疑似）

Cherry Springs State Park, PA, USA 美国宾夕法尼亚州樱桃泉州立公园 · August 2026 2026年八月

Nikon D7500 · 24mm · f/2.8 · 10s · ISO 3200 Nikon D7500 · 24mm · f/2.8 · 10s · ISO 3200

It isn't a satellite, because the streak isn't a straight line; it should be a meteor, because the streak is thin at the head and thick at the tail, and the point of light at the tail is big and bright. We also saw a fireball that night — had I caught it on camera, it would have been a colored streak of light. 不是一颗卫星因为它不是一条笔直的线；应该是一颗流星因为线条头细尾粗，同时尾巴上的光点很大很闪。当天还看到了火流星，如果能拍到的话会是一道有颜色的光线。
