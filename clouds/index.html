Preface

前言

Looking up through every season, I am grateful for these endlessly varied clouds — they remind me how vast and beautiful this world is. While sorting through the clouds I have seen (references: The Cloud Collector's Handbook and the International Cloud Atlas), I realized that many clouds are unremarkable yet still worth collecting, that many are wondrous but fleeting, and that some appear only at certain latitudes and under extreme weather — so rare you might wait a lifetime to be lucky enough to catch one or two. I will keep looking up, and keep recording and collecting the clouds I see. Since I am not a professional meteorologist and rely only on field guides and Claude to identify cloud types, there may be some mistaken classifications on this page. If you spot one, please reach out and let me know — I will correct it.

一年四季仰望看到的形态各异的云朵们，感谢它们的存在让我知道这个世界有这么地大这么地美好。 在分类过往看到的云的过程中（参考：《云彩收集者手册》《国际云图集》），我也发现还有很多云不起眼但也值得收集， 有很多云奇妙却转瞬即逝，还有些云只在特定的经纬度和极端的天气条件下才会出现，需要一辈子的时间才能幸运地碰上那么一两次。 我会继续仰望天空，继续记录和收集看到的各式各样的云彩。 同时因为我不是专业气象学家，辨别云朵类型依靠的只有图鉴和 Claude，所以这个网页里可能会有一些错误的分类。 如果你看到了可以联系我进行指正，我会修改。

1.1Cumulus积云

Cumulus 积云

Everglades National Park, Florida, USA 美国佛罗里达州大沼泽地国家公园 · March 2026 2026年三月 · Common 常见

Cumulus clouds met while cycling through Everglades National Park — great cottony tufts scattered across the sky. Riding beneath one gave a brief moment of shade from the direct sun. 在大沼泽地国家公园骑行途中遇到的积云，大朵大朵地像棉絮一样散落在天空中， 骑行到云朵底下时可以短暂躲避太阳的直射。

Cumulus humilis 淡积云

Florida, USA 美国佛罗里达州 · August 2024 2024年八月 · Common 常见

A field of cumulus humilis scattered between the mountains — fair weather, good for taking off and landing. 散落在山脉间的淡积云场，是一个起飞降落的好天气。

Cumulus congestus 浓积云

Big Island, Hawaii, USA 美国夏威夷州大岛 · December 2024 2024年十二月 · Common 常见

A heavy cumulus pressing down on the summit as we climbed the volcano. Hard to tell whether the air truly felt thick and damp, or whether we were just worn out from the hike. 爬上火山顶时看到山上压着的一朵厚重的积云，分不清是我们爬山爬累了觉得空气有些厚重， 还是风确实湿润沉重。

1.2Stratocumulus层积云

Stratocumulus 层积云

Western Sichuan, China 中国川西 · August 2023 2023年八月 · Common 常见

Near Tagong Monastery in western Sichuan it rained on and off. Standing on the hilltop, I felt closer to the base of the clouds than to the ground below. 车开到川西的塔公寺附近时，天在间间断断地下雨。站在山顶上看， 相较于地面来说，感觉自己离云朵底部更近一些。

Stratocumulus 层积云

Miami, Florida, USA 美国佛罗里达州迈阿密 · March 2026 2026年三月 · Common 常见

A spring-break trip to Miami that unexpectedly ran into stormy weather. Walking along the beach and watching the gulls get tossed about by the wind turned out to be its own kind of fun. 春假去迈阿密玩没想到遇上了风暴天，在海滩边走走， 看看海鸥被大风吹得四仰八叉也很有意思。

1.3Stratus层云

Stratus 层云

Western Sichuan, China 中国川西 · August 2023 2023年八月 · Common 常见

A cloud drifting out of the valley like cooking smoke. I'm not sure it really is stratus, but since stratus is the lowest of all clouds, I've placed it in this category for now. 像炊烟一样从山谷里飘出来的云，不确定是不是层云， 但是因为层云是所有云中高度最低的，所以先放在这个分类里。

Section 4:Multiple families多个云族

4.1Cumulonimbus积雨云

Cumulonimbus 积雨云

Yellowstone National Park, USA 美国黄石国家公园 · August 2024 2024年八月 · Common 常见

A perfect cumulonimbus, with the rain pouring beneath it looking a little like the stalk of a mushroom. 一朵完美的积雨云，下面倾泻的雨有一点像蘑菇的菇柄。

Mammatus beneath a cumulonimbus 积雨云的悬球状云底部

Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, USA 美国宾夕法尼亚州费城 · June 2024 2024年六月 · Rare 罕见

After a torrential downpour at dusk, half-spheres hung down from the base of the cloud. The setting sun stained them an apocalyptic color — stunning and unreal. 黄昏暴雨过后，从积云底部悬吊下来一个个半球， 将坠的太阳给它们染上末日一般的颜色，震撼且不真实。

Anvil top of a cumulonimbus 积雨云的砧状顶部

Las Vegas, Nevada, USA 美国内华达州拉斯维加斯 · August 2025 2025年八月 · Somewhat uncommon 较为少见

An anvil cloud in the distance, seen as the plane descended on its approach to Las Vegas. The vast, empty desert robbed even this enormous cloud of any sense of scale, making it feel as if the far-off cloud and the ground were close enough to touch. 飞机从云上降下，快要抵达拉斯维加斯时看到的远处的一片砧状云。 空旷的一望无际的沙漠让巨大的云也失去了 scale， 让人感觉远处的云和地面只要伸手就能够到。
